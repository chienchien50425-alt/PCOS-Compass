# AI Ladies Lounge Hackathon 2025 - PCOS Compass

Team Member: Nadeen Al-Aubadi, Wei Ling Chien, Chaw Su Su Thinn

## The problem we observed
PCOS affects 1 in 10 women and is one of the leading causes of infertility.
Symptoms are irregular and multi-system (cycles, weight, acne, mood), but
current tracking tools are fragmented and clinicians only see isolated snapshots.
This gap delays diagnosis and makes care less effective.

## The solution we propose/built
We developed PCOS Compass, an AI-powered tracker that unifies daily logs and
wearable data, then generates monthly summaries for women and their doctors.
This turns scattered information into clear trends and actionable insights,
empowering patients and improving clinical care.

Link: https://pcos-compass-guide.lovable.app/
